Rubik’s Cube / A personal story

The Art of Solving

I started learning the Rubik’s Cube at around 16, with YouTube tutorials and a lot of repeated attempts. Learning independently meant slowing down, remembering patterns and practising the same steps until they began to make sense.

What keeps me interested is the balance between recognising a familiar pattern and working out what to do next. Memorisation helps, but so does paying attention to the way each move changes the puzzle.

My best solve was approximately 17 seconds — an unusually lucky solve, rather than an official competition record. I usually average around 30 seconds. Improving those everyday solves is what makes practice satisfying.

Guitar & Music / A personal story

Finding My Rhythm

I began learning guitar at around 13, with guidance from my older brother. He helped me get started, and I continued learning independently, exploring both acoustic and electric guitars.

Practice has taught me that progress often comes from returning to a difficult passage, listening carefully and trying again. Music gives me a creative outlet away from programming, with a different kind of focus.

Performing at school also gave me a chance to become more comfortable playing in front of people. I value the patience and confidence that come from learning an instrument as much as the songs themselves.

2004 Proton Wira / A personal story

More Than Just a Car

A 2004 Proton Wira, passed from my father to me. Its story is about family, responsibility and the gradual work of becoming more independent.

Chapter 01

The Family Car

This 2004 Proton Wira originally belonged to my father. It was part of our everyday life for years, carrying the family to school and work. These photographs are part of that car’s story.

Chapter 02

Bringing It Back to Life

When the Wira was no longer in good condition and was not being used regularly, it was passed down to me. I used money from part-time work to repair and improve it gradually, prioritising mechanical reliability and engine-related problems before its appearance. These photographs show different stages of work on the car, including workshop visits and the interior.

Chapter 03

The Transformation

The changes happened a little at a time, as I could afford them. Seeing the Wira take shape taught me to be patient and make practical choices. The result matters to me because of the work and responsibility behind it.

Chapter 04

The Journey Continues

Today, the Wira helps me get to college and work. It is a connection to my family, and a reminder that independence is built through small, consistent efforts.

“It once carried my father's dreams. Today, it carries mine.”

Photography & Filming / A personal story

Behind the Lens

Filming for Abby | Foodie Bahau

I help my older sister, Abby, film food-review content, handling the camera while she creates the content and handles the video editing. My part involves framing shots, trying different camera angles, and recording scenes and food.

It made me look more carefully at ordinary details: where the light falls, what belongs in the frame and how a change of angle affects the scene. Getting a useful shot also meant communicating with my sister about what she needed.

I enjoyed contributing to the filming process and learning how a sequence of simple observations can become visual storytelling. The editing and ownership of the content remained with my sister.
