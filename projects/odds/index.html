Case study 01 / Individual Final Year Project

On-Demand Delivery Management System.

A delivery management platform bringing Customer, Driver and Admin workflows into one application.

Technologies

OverviewFeaturesDevelopmentGallery

01 / Context

Project overview.

I am building ODDS as my individual final year project. It brings delivery information into separate workspaces for customers, drivers and administrators, so each role has a clearer place to manage its part of a delivery.

Customers create delivery requests, drivers review their work and vehicles, and administrators oversee applications and operational records. The project is still in development; the screens below document its current state.

02 / In the application

Key features.

  1. 01

    Customer requests

    A delivery form collects pickup and drop-off details, with a map interface for choosing locations.

  2. 02

    Driver workspace

    The driver dashboard brings together availability, available requests, current work and delivery history.

  3. 03

    Vehicle management

    Drivers can view an active vehicle alongside registered vehicles and their approval states.

  4. 04

    Administration

    The admin workspace presents driver applications, vehicle reviews and delivery records.

  5. 05

    Delivery status and reassignment

    The customer overview displays request status, including a pre-pickup cancellation notice and an Awaiting Reassignment state.

03 / Building & learning

Development journey.

Laravel 12, PHP and Blade provide the web application structure, with MySQL for its records. Organising screens around the three user roles helps keep customer tasks, driver work and administration distinct.

The project has also required careful attention to how requests, vehicles and approval states are presented. I am continuing to develop and check these workflows rather than treating every screen as a finished feature.

Main challenge

Database Management & Recovery

Working through database configuration and migration challenges taught me the importance of structured backups, controlled changes and recovery procedures. It has made me more deliberate about preparing changes and protecting existing data.

05 / Looking back

What I’m taking forward.

ODDS is teaching me to think beyond an individual screen: a change in one role can affect what another role sees. My focus is to keep refining the workflows while becoming more careful with database changes.
